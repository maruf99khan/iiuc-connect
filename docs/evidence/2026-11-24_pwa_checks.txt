Built while the owner was away; nothing here needed their accounts or secrets.

What was built
  app/manifest.ts   served at /manifest.webmanifest: name and short name "IIUC Connect",
                    start_url /dashboard, scope /, display standalone, theme colour
                    #0f5132 (the page's own), icons 192 and 512 plus a maskable 512.
  public/icons/     icon-192.png, icon-512.png (rounded), icon-maskable-512.png (full
                    bleed, letters inside the 80% safe zone). app/apple-icon.png (180).
  No service worker, by decision D-024: caching signed-in pages on a shared phone would
  keep a member's data after they sign out. The app needs a connection to work.

  python e2e/security_anon.py   all required headers present.
